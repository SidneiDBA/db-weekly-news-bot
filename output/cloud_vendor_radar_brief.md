## ⚠️ Operational Risks:
1. One significant operational risk associated with these solutions is vendor lock-in. Moving from one platform to another can be complex and costly due to the proprietary nature of their offerings. Another potential issue is security, as data stored on cloud platforms may require proper protection and encryption during processing. Additionally, scalability risks include performance bottlenecks, limited data throughput, or unstable connections, which could disrupt workflows and cause service interruptions.

## 🧪 What To Test This Week:
1. A junior DBA working with these solutions can start by testing the compatibility between databases and AI services in their cloud environment. This can include verifying database connection from various AI platforms or evaluating the efficiency of different database systems to handle data processing tasks for specific AI applications. Additionally, monitoring and optimizing cloud resource utilization is crucial to ensure cost-effectiveness and prevent any potential scaling issues.

## 📚 Skills To Learn Next:

Finally, learning to optimize data pipelines between database and AI systems for efficient data processing, analysis, and decision-making can enhance the overall cloud platform capabilities.
